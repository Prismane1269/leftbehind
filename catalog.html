Abandoned but not Forgotten · Space Apps Challenge 2026

Eighteen machines. Six on the Moon, ten on Mars, two leaving the solar system.

Each card tells you where the machine sits, whether it is still talking, and what its science changed. Open one to turn the hardware in 3D, read the story, and jump to the NASA page behind every fact.
